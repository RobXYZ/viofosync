The percentage above is measured against the free space the operating system reports. Inside a Synology shared folder, or a ZFS dataset with a quota, that figure describes the whole underlying volume rather than the slice this folder may fill, so retention would never fire. Declare the size here and the percentage counts against your quota instead.
